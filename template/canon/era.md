# Era

The story is set in the present day. The in-world calendar runs roughly parallel to the real world, with the city's session-1 start date pinned during the first playthrough.

## What this means in practice

- Phones, EVs, remote work, e-commerce, ride-share, climate anxiety, post-pandemic civic budgets — all in play.
- No speculative tech. No autonomous vehicle utopias. Self-driving exists at the level it does in 2026.
- Federal and state context exists in the background but is rarely a direct character — this is a local story.
- Money is in current US/Canadian dollars depending on which side of the border the city sits.

## Time passes

Default 2–6 in-world months pass between real-world play sessions. A multi-year story arc takes many sessions to play out. Long construction projects (a stadium, a light rail line) span multiple sessions — and so do scandals, careers, and grudges.
